repository, and no trademark rights are granted here.

You may:

* keep them in place when you build and run the applications of this repository
  unmodified, for your own use.

You may not:

* use them in a way that suggests that the institutions endorse, support or have
  reviewed your work;
* keep them in a modified or rebranded distribution of the applications. Replace
  them with your own artwork instead. The applications look for the files by name,
  so replacing the image files is enough; no code change is needed.
